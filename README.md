# Workflow Hardware Advisor

I want to start planning out an assistant who can help me and other users to plan out what hardware they need to handle their specific workflows. No noise, no marketing fluff, just a robust analysis of their workflow and then find the right hardware at the right price for them. Make it specific. In the future this has an agentic commerce application but for now it will just be recommendations. This will span from the student who needs a lightweight and fast laptop to the power user who wants a 24/7 ai agent on a desktop. RAM considerations and processor etc are hard for a lot of people to understand particularly the generations considerations. Let's plan this rather than jump into build
